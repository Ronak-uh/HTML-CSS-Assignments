Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that lived millions of years ago during the Mesozoic Era, which is divided into the Triassic, Jurassic, and Cretaceous periods. They came in all shapes and sizes—from the towering, plant-eating Brachiosaurus to the fierce, meat-eating Tyrannosaurus rex. Dinosaurs ruled the land for over 160 million years before mysteriously going extinct around 65 million years ago, likely due to a massive asteroid impact and volcanic activity. Fossils of dinosaurs have been discovered all over the world, helping scientists understand Earth’s prehistoric life and the process of evolution. Today, birds are considered the closest living relatives of dinosaurs, keeping their legacy alive.
