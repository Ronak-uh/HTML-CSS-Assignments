Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam, known as the "Missile Man of India," was an aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born on October 15, 1931, and made significant contributions to India’s civilian space and military missile programs. Loved by millions, Dr. Kalam inspired countless young minds to dream big and pursue knowledge and innovation.

Qualities & Achievements

  • Key figure in India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998
  • Developed ballistic missiles under the IGMDP program
  • President of India from 2002 to 2007
  • Known for his humility, vision, and love for students
  • Author of the best-selling book "Wings of Fire"

Favorite Quotes

  • "Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
  • "If you want to shine like a sun, first burn like a sun."
  • "Don’t take rest after your first victory because if you fail in second, more lips are waiting to say that your first victory was just luck."
